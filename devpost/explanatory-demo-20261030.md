## Narration, one English take

**Scene 1**

Your finance team wants AI agents to handle invoices. But a fake recipient or an unusual charge can turn automation into a very expensive mistake. MandateGate is the payment control layer between an AI agent and PayPal.

**Scene 2**

Let's start with a supplier invoice. I upload a test PDF. MandateGate saves the original and shows which details need checking. AI can help read the document and flag suspicious content, but it cannot change a trusted payee or approve a payment. The reviewer can compare extracted fields with the original PDF. In another saved test, GLM flags suspicious instructions inside an invoice.

**Scene 3**

Next, the operations agent submits three test payment requests for Northstar Components. MandateGate checks each request against vendor records and company policy. Watch the decision engine apply the same rules to every case.

**Scene 4**

First, twelve hundred dollars to the trusted recipient. The amount is normal. The destination matches. MandateGate returns ALLOW, with the reasons and evidence available for inspection.

**Scene 5**

Second, twenty-eight hundred dollars. This is above the vendor's normal twenty-five-hundred-dollar limit. MandateGate marks ESCALATE, putting the request in front of a human instead of paying automatically. Watch the reviewer enter a reason and decline it. The payment stays stopped.

**Scene 6**

Third, an ordinary amount, but a different payment address. That's a common invoice-redirection risk. MandateGate returns DENY and keeps the new recipient untrusted.

**Scene 7**

PayPal handles execution. For a properly authorized request, only MandateGate's protected server-side executor can submit a payout. Here is an actual successful PayPal Sandbox integration test, showing the payout identifiers and verified webhook used to confirm its final status.

**Scene 8**

This is MandateGate: AI helps process invoices. Policy controls payment authority. PayPal executes approved transfers, with an evidence trail for finance and security teams. Let agents do the work without giving them the keys to your money.
